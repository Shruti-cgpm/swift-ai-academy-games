Wrap-up

The whole loop has 6 steps.

You will use it every time you use AI in this course. Still not right after step 4? Go back to step 3, and after 2 moves start again at step 1.

Your task. Put the 6 steps in the right order.

  1. Ask with all 4 parts: Context, Role, Task, Format.
  2. Read the answer.
  3. Name the problem.
  4. Use one move.
  5. Check it with a source you trust.
  6. Use it.

Why does a short request get a general answer? Tell your buddy, or write it here.

Your task. Write one sentence, or tap I told my buddy.

Compare with a model answer

Because the AI tool guesses the most likely words. Every gap I leave gets a common guess, and common guesses make a general answer.
