Session run sheet

The session runs for 5 hours in 8 blocks.

In every block, talk time is shorter than practice time on purpose. Each block has its own page after this one.

    Demo script · 1 of 3

    Start the controlled fabrication demo with this line.

    Use the industrial visit note. Learners see the same fictional record later. Say the gold lines exactly, and use your own words for the rest.

    "I will show you a note that an AI tool wrote. Some of it is right. Some of it is wrong on purpose, so you can see how checking really works."

    Demo script · 2 of 3

    Show the AI draft and model the check.

    Show the AI draft on the screen: "On 10 August, 35 trainees from Batch 5A visited Bansal Auto Components. The plant runs a fully automated line needing no manual checks, as confirmed by the visit register."

    "Watch what I do in the next 30 seconds. I mark every fact, figure, date, name and source. Then I separate what the register really supports from what it does not."

    Demo script · 3 of 3

    Reveal the real register and correct the draft.

    Show the real register live. It shows 32 trainees and 1 worn drill bit, and it says nothing about automation. Correct the figure, remove the unsupported claim and keep the source line honest.

    Your task. Answer one question to rehearse the correction.

    "Notice that I did not guess. I checked, then I corrected, qualified or removed. That is the whole routine. Now you will do the same with your own record."

    The register says nothing about automation. What do you do with that claim?

    Wrong-answer-in-class protocol

    Follow these steps when the live tool gives a wrong answer.

    Use these steps for an unplanned wrong answer. Do not use them for the demo, which is wrong on purpose.

    Pause and name it out loud.Say: "That answer does not look right. We will not just accept it. This is exactly the moment this course is about."
    Mark it live, with the class.Ask: "What in this answer needs checking? A figure, a date, a name?" Let the class mark it. Do not do it alone.

    Wrong-answer protocol · step 3

    Step 3 is to check the answer honestly.

    Check it against a real record, or say honestly that you cannot.If a record is at hand, verify it together. If not, say plainly: "I will verify this and confirm next session." Then do it.
    Learners may look anxious or start to distrust all AI answers. Tell them this is normal AI behaviour, not a broken tool. It is exactly why the marking routine exists.

    Wrong-answer protocol · step 4

    Step 4 is to turn the moment into evidence.

    Your task. Put the 4 protocol steps in the right order.

    Turn it into evidence, not just a moment.Note what happened in your facilitator log. If it affects a graded task, follow your escalation path. Do not decide alone.
    1. Pause and name it out loud.
    2. Mark it live, with the class.
    3. Check it against a real record, or say so honestly.
    4. Turn it into evidence in your log.
